Buffers: late December/January if targeting an ICLR workshop. The suggested ICLR contribution deadline is not an individual workshop deadline. EACL SRW direct submission is listed as Dec 15, so the last week cannot be assumed available for that route; recheck the actual CFP.

Cuts: defer mechanisms, new architectures, interaction matrices and broad sweeps first. Never cut ordinary/reference comparisons, intervention validation, factual labels or uncertainty reporting. Select at most one extension; all are optional.

Reading alongside work: TOFU/OpenUnlearning and cache source in weeks 1–2; both anchor papers by week 3; closest-paper full-text screening before claims freeze. Recheck literature after the pilot and before submission.
